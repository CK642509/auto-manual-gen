# Live Monitoring

Live monitoring is the default screen when you open DemoStreamApp. This chapter explains how to find a camera and place its live view on the layout.

## Steps

1. Wait for the **Cameras** list on the left to finish loading.

{{screenshot:live-monitor-01}}

2. In the **{{legend.search}}**, type **lobby**.
3. In the filtered list, double-click **Lobby-01**.

{{screenshot:live-monitor-02}}

## Result

The live view of **Lobby-01** fills the first empty cell on the right, shown as **{{legend.cell}}** in the figure. A **Lobby-01 added to cell 1** notification also appears in the lower-right corner.

To replace the camera in a cell, click **{{legend.remove}}** in the upper-right corner of that cell. Other cells are not affected. To change the number of cells, use the **{{legend.layout}}** at the top. To keep the current arrangement, click **{{legend.save}}**. See the **Saving a Layout** chapter for details.

> Note: Double-clicking always fills the first empty cell. When the layout is full, the message **Layout is full — remove a cell or switch to a larger layout** appears. Remove a cell first, or switch to a larger layout.
