# Interface Overview

When you open DemoStreamApp, it starts on the **Live Monitoring** tab. This chapter explains where the three main areas of the screen are and what they are for.

## Steps

1. Wait for the **Cameras** list on the left to finish loading.

{{screenshot:overview-01}}

## Result

The screen is divided into three areas:

- **{{legend.stats}}**: shows **Cameras Online**, **Open Alerts**, and **Inference FPS**.
- **{{legend.cameras}}**: lists every camera. You can search by name or zone, or click **Add Camera**.
- **{{legend.grid}}**: shows the live view of each camera. Use **Layout** and **Saved layout** at the top to change the grid.

Double-click a camera in **{{legend.cameras}}** to place its live view in the next empty cell of **{{legend.grid}}**. See the **Live Monitoring** chapter for details.
