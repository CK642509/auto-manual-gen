# Video Analytics Settings

Video analytics determines which detections the system runs on camera footage. This chapter explains how to turn on face recognition.

## Steps

1. Click the **Settings** tab at the top of the screen.
2. In the **Video Analytics** section, turn on the **{{legend.face}}**.

{{screenshot:settings-01}}

3. Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the page.

## Result

The notification **Settings saved** appears, which means the settings have been saved.

After the **{{legend.face}}** is turned on, **{{legend.threshold}}** appears below it. The default value is 75%.

The **{{legend.motion}}** is on by default, so you do not need to change it.

> Note: **{{legend.threshold}}** is shown only when the **{{legend.face}}** is on.
